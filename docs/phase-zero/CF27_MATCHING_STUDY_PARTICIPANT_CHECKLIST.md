# CF27 Matching Study Participant Checklist

**Status:** checklist only
**Study status:** NOT STARTED

Before the scan:

- Confirm you meet the age requirement for the study.
- Confirm you are scanning yourself or have permission from the person being scanned.
- Review the current-session face analysis consent.
- Remove glasses and headwear when practical.
- Use soft, even lighting.
- Keep a neutral expression.
- Keep hair away from your face, jaw, and ears when practical.

Required capture views:

- Front view.
- Left 45-degree view.
- Right 45-degree view.
- Left profile.
- Right profile.

After recommendations:

- Review the original top-three recommendation.
- Build the best candidate in College Football 27 when asked by the operator.
- Rate usefulness from 1 to 5.
- Rate resemblance from 1 to 5.
- Identify the main mismatch reason if none of the top three are useful.
- Confirm whether screenshot refinement helped when performed.
- Confirm raw media and temporary profile deletion before the result is finalized.

Do not submit names, contact information, gamer tags, or account credentials in study worksheets.
