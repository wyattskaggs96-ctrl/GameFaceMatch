# Tester Consent Draft

Status: Draft for owner and legal review. This is not legal advice.

## Plain-Language Consent

I understand that GameFace Match is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by EA SPORTS, Electronic Arts, or College Football 27.

I understand that GameFace Match recommends the closest available in-game appearance settings. It does not directly import my face into College Football 27.

## Camera and Image Use

I consent to using my browser camera or uploaded images for the current local recommendation session.

I understand that the web MVP uses RGB images only. It does not use native iPhone TrueDepth, ARKit, depth geometry, or 3D reconstruction.

## Local Processing

I understand that image processing is intended to happen locally in my browser. The app must not upload my face images as part of the current MVP.

I understand that the app does not identify me and does not create identity embeddings.

## Sensitive Traits

I understand that the app must not infer ethnicity, health, personality, intelligence, attractiveness, criminality, or other sensitive traits.

## Temporary Data

I understand that raw capture images are temporary session data and are deleted by default. I can delete active session data from the Privacy Center.

## Derived Profile

I consent separately before saving any derived face-profile information. A derived profile should contain standardized measurements, quality summaries, and user-confirmed appearance attributes, not raw face images.

## Saved Builds

I consent separately before saving a completed build. Saved builds should store non-image settings and catalog version details by default.

## Screenshots

I consent separately before using screenshot-refinement intake. I understand screenshot refinement is unavailable until real comparison logic and verified catalog data exist.

## Future Product Improvement

Future product-improvement participation is not enabled in the current MVP. I do not consent to product-improvement use unless a separate future consent flow is implemented.

## Future Model Training

Future model-training participation is not enabled in the current MVP. I do not consent to model-training use unless a separate future consent flow is implemented.

## Deletion

I understand that I can delete local GameFace Match data from the Privacy Center. I understand deletion claims apply to local browser data controlled by the current MVP and do not cover screenshots or files I separately saved outside the app.

## Withdrawal

I may stop testing at any time and delete my local data.

## Minors

Tester participation by minors requires owner-approved guardian consent before any beta begins.
