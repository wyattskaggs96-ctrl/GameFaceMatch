# Counsel Review Checklist

**Status:** Draft checklist for licensed counsel. Not legal advice.

## Positioning and Affiliation

- [ ] Review independent companion positioning.
- [ ] Approve or revise EA, EA SPORTS, College Football 27, console, NCAA, CLC, school, and related trademark disclaimers.
- [ ] Confirm where disclaimers must appear.
- [ ] Confirm whether social posts and creator materials need abbreviated disclaimers.
- [ ] Confirm whether the product name creates confusion risk.

## Game Screenshots and Evidence

- [ ] Decide whether game screenshots may be used publicly.
- [ ] Decide whether internal research galleries may display game imagery to contractors or testers.
- [ ] Decide whether screenshot thumbnails, cropped derivatives, or contact sheets have different restrictions from full screenshots.
- [ ] Confirm evidence retention and access controls for game recordings.
- [ ] Confirm user-uploaded screenshot terms.

## Biometric and Privacy

- [ ] Determine whether raw face images, landmarks, normalized ratios, and derived profiles are biometric data in target jurisdictions.
- [ ] Review consent wording for camera use, local face analysis, temporary processing, and derived-profile saving.
- [ ] Review retention policy and deletion commitments.
- [ ] Review no-upload, no-identity-recognition, no-sensitive-inference language.
- [ ] Review future cloud backup, analytics, product improvement, and model-training consent requirements.

## Child and Teen Privacy

- [ ] Set minimum age for independent use.
- [ ] Define parent or guardian consent requirements.
- [ ] Decide whether minors may join beta tests.
- [ ] Review process for scanning someone else with permission.
- [ ] Review parent/guardian deletion request process.

## Terms and Consumer Protection

- [ ] Draft or approve terms of use.
- [ ] Draft or approve privacy policy.
- [ ] Review limitation of liability and no-guarantee language.
- [ ] Review support process and contact requirements.
- [ ] Review jurisdiction and dispute terms.
- [ ] Review consumer cancellation/deletion rights.

## Advertising and Claims

- [ ] Review landing page.
- [ ] Review onboarding and app copy.
- [ ] Review social posts and video scripts.
- [ ] Review beta tester guide.
- [ ] Review paid-feature language before charging.
- [ ] Confirm no claim of perfect match, direct face import, official integration, guaranteed resemblance, biometric identification, or medical-grade measurement appears affirmatively.

## App Store, Web, and Accessibility

- [ ] Review web disclosures for camera permission, secure context, RGB-only capture, and local-only processing.
- [ ] Review PWA installability language.
- [ ] Review future iOS App Store privacy labels and listing text.
- [ ] Review accessibility statement and support process.
- [ ] Review paid feature disclosures, refund policy, tax responsibilities, and purchase restoration before payment integration.
