# Catalog Review Guide

NOT PRODUCTION DATA
NOT A VERIFIED GAME RECORD

The review workflow protects against invented or misread College Football 27 options.

## First Review

- Confirm the visible label or index exactly matches the screenshot evidence.
- Confirm platform, game version, patch, game mode, and Road to Glory creation path.
- Confirm all five required screenshots are present and named correctly.
- Confirm capture conditions are recorded.
- Confirm menu instructions reach the intended option.
- Reject records with placeholders or guessed values.

## Second Review

- Must be completed by a different reviewer.
- Re-walk the menu instructions from the live game or retained video evidence.
- Confirm that no option was reordered, renamed, retired, or introduced by a patch.
- Confirm that publication would not expose test fixtures or template records.
- Follow `docs/SECOND_VERIFIER_RUNBOOK.md` for independent counts, menu remapping, native-order verification, file and front-view checks, deterministic secondary-angle sampling, mismatch classification, recapture requirements, final acknowledgment, allowed statuses, and prohibited shortcuts.

## Verification

A record may become `verified` only after both reviews approve it, menu navigation evidence is present, and package validation passes.
