# Head Template Faces 001-029 Research Candidates

NOT PRODUCTION DATA  
NOT A VERIFIED GAME RECORD

This package records directly selected Head Template labels Face 1 through Face 29 from the two current Head Template Xbox screen recordings.

It intentionally does not claim that 29 is the complete Head Template count. Later labels are outside this package's scope and must be handled by a separate evidence-ingestion task before any catalog count can be asserted.

Key constraints:

- Face 12 is one catalog identity with evidence from both Head Template recordings.
- Records are primary-research candidates only.
- Verification state remains `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- No record in this folder may be loaded as production catalog data.
- Hair, facial hair, eye-black, and obstruction notes are visual QA observations only, not native College Football 27 option labels.
- Elevated and lowered views are missing from the current recordings.
