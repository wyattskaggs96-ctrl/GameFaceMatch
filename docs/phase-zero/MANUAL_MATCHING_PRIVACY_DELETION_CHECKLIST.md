# Manual Matching Privacy And Deletion Checklist

**Status:** required study checklist
**Default raw-media retention:** delete after review

## Before Capture

- Confirm consent version.
- Confirm participant is the person shown or has permission.
- Assign a pseudonymous participant ID.
- Confirm no participant name, email, gamer tag, or school affiliation will be written to repository-safe rows.

## During Processing

- Keep raw photos in the approved local study workspace only.
- Do not upload raw photos.
- Do not add raw photos, screenshots of participant faces, or precise facial measurements to analytics.
- Record only non-image study metadata in CSV rows.

## After Review

- Delete temporary raw photos unless separate explicit retention consent exists.
- Delete temporary derived profile if the participant requests deletion or when retention period ends.
- Record deletion timestamp and verifier.
- Confirm anonymized exports include no raw media, direct identifiers, or precise facial measurements.

## Withdrawal

If a participant withdraws before finalization, stop processing, delete raw media, mark the private working record withdrawn, and do not include the participant in metrics.
