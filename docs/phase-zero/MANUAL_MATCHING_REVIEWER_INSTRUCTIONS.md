# Manual Matching Reviewer Instructions

**Status:** reviewer workflow only
**Allowed data:** verified production catalog records only

## Reviewer Rules

- Review participants independently before seeing another reviewer's rankings.
- Use only verified College Football 27 catalog records.
- Do not invent game labels, menu paths, or option numbers.
- Do not use race, ethnicity, attractiveness, identity, health, personality, or age labels.
- Do not store or copy raw participant media outside the approved local study workspace.

## Review Steps

1. Confirm all five participant reference views are present.
2. Confirm capture quality, visible face, neutral expression where practical, and one-person visibility.
3. Compare the participant's reference views to the verified catalog.
4. Record top-three head recommendations in rank order.
5. Record hair and facial-hair choices only when those categories are verified.
6. Record objective reasons and mismatch reason codes.
7. Submit your worksheet before viewing another reviewer's choices.
8. After both reviews are complete, compare top-choice and top-three-set agreement.

## Output

Use `data/phase-zero/manual_matching_reviews.template.csv`. Leave raw media out of the worksheet.
