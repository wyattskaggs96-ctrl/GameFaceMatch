# Manual Matching Participant Instructions

**Status:** study instructions only
**Participant names required:** No
**Raw media committed to repository:** No

## What You Are Helping Test

You are helping GameFace Match evaluate whether its verified College Football 27 recommendations are useful. This is an independent companion product. It does not import your face into the game, identify you, or infer sensitive traits.

## What You Will Do

1. Review and acknowledge the study consent.
2. Complete the guided face capture or approved upload flow.
3. Confirm that only one person is visible and the images are usable.
4. Let the study operator record the app's original top-three recommendations.
5. Review the recommended in-game build options after reviewers complete their notes.
6. Choose which result you would actually use, if any.
7. Rate usefulness and resemblance from 1 to 5.
8. Confirm raw-media and derived-profile deletion.

## Required Capture Views

- Straight-on
- Left 45 degrees
- Right 45 degrees
- Left profile
- Right profile

Use even lighting, a neutral expression, no face-altering filters, and no hats or glasses where practical.

## Privacy

The worksheet uses only a pseudonymous participant ID such as `participant-study-v1-001`. Do not write names, email addresses, gamer tags, school names, or direct identifiers in study rows. Raw photos are temporary by default and are deleted after review unless a separate written consent says otherwise.
