# Resemblance Rating Rubric

Use this rubric only after verified College Football 27 catalog records are loaded. Do not use it to claim identity probability.

Recommended wording:

Match score based on the game's available appearance options.

Do not ask testers whether the app identified them. The app must not perform identity recognition.

## Top-One Rating

Rate the top result from 1 to 5:

1. Not useful: the result does not resemble the intended appearance.
2. Weak: a few broad traits are in the right direction, but the result would not be used.
3. Acceptable starting point: the result is usable with manual adjustment.
4. Good: the result is close enough that only minor adjustments are needed.
5. Excellent: the result is the best available in-game starting point.

## Top-Three Usefulness Rating

Rate whether at least one of the top-three results is useful:

1. None of the three are useful.
2. One option has a small amount of value but is not acceptable.
3. At least one option is an acceptable starting point.
4. At least one option is good and easy to adjust.
5. The top three give clear, useful alternatives.

## Build-Guide Correctness

Rate build instructions only when they come from verified menu paths:

1. Incorrect or impossible to follow.
2. Mostly unclear or missing important steps.
3. Usable with some confusion.
4. Clear and accurate.
5. Clear, accurate, and easy to follow near the game console.

## Confidence Explanation

Rate whether the explanation is understandable:

1. Misleading or confusing.
2. Too technical or unclear.
3. Understandable with some uncertainty.
4. Clear and honest.
5. Clear, honest, and helpful for choosing among options.

## Free-Text Prompts

Ask:

- Which result would you try first in the game?
- Which result looked least useful?
- What was the biggest visible mismatch?
- Did the explanation make uncertainty clear?
- Did any wording feel like identity recognition or overclaiming?

Do not ask testers to rate ethnicity, health, attractiveness, age, personality, or other sensitive traits.
